Meal Planner & Recipe Keeper - Stashcook

Stashcook is a meal planner and recipe keeper that helps you save recipes, plan your week, and organize shopping in one place.

It’s a simple weekly meal planner for everyday cooking - without the clutter.


💾 Recipe Keeper for every recipe you save

Stashcook is a recipe keeper built for real life.
Save recipes from TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest, YouTube, Facebook, BBC Good Food, Food Network, AllRecipes, Yummly, and almost any website.

You can also add recipes from screenshots, photos, handwritten cards, or notes.
Everything lives in one organized recipe box, ready when you need it.

This is your digital recipe book - searchable, editable, and always with you.
